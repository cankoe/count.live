Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Short version: count.live doesn't collect, store, or sell any personal data. No accounts, no cookies. Privacy-preserving analytics are provided by Cloudflare — no individual user data is exposed.

No Sign-up Required

You can create, customize, and share countdowns without creating an account or providing any personal information.

Data We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal data ourselves. All countdown configuration and calculations happen in your browser or on your device.

Cookies & Local Storage

This website does not use cookies. Your browser's local storage is used solely to remember your theme preference (light/dark mode) and your recent countdown history — this data never leaves your device.

Analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page views and performance. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookie-free and privacy-preserving — it collects only aggregated metrics (visit counts, page views, load times) and does not track individual users, build behavioral profiles, or share data with advertisers.

We do not use Google Analytics or any other third-party tracking service. The iOS app does not include any analytics SDK.

Cloudflare Hosting

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare may collect standard infrastructure logs (IP addresses, browser type, request metadata) as part of operating their network. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for details.

iOS App

The count.live iOS app stores your saved countdowns locally on your device using iOS's standard storage (UserDefaults / shared app group). This data is not transmitted to any server. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device and are never sent to our servers.

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

URL Sharing

When you share a countdown URL, the event configuration (date, title, colors, etc.) is encoded in the URL itself. Be mindful of any personal information you include in countdown titles or subtitles if sharing publicly.

Background Images

If you enter a background image URL, that image is fetched directly by your browser or device from the URL you provide. We do not proxy, cache, or process those images on our servers.

Children's Privacy

count.live does not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 13. Since we collect no personal data from anyone, this service is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at dev@count.live.
